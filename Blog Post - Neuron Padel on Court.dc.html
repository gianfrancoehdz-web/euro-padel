From Experience to the Next Generation: Neuron Padel on Court

Padel equipment is ultimately tested in one place: the court.

For Neuron Padel, working alongside competitive players means following that process at very different stages of a sporting career. From the experience of professional competition with Carlos Martí Vaño to the international development of young talents Mattia Settimi and Zara Settimi, three different journeys are being played with the same objective: keep improving.

Carlos Martí Vaño: experience built through competition

Carlos Martí Vaño brings years of high-level competition to the Neuron project.

The Spanish player has competed on some of padel's biggest stages, including appearances in the main draws of Premier Padel events in Rome and Milan. In 2024, alongside Miguel Solbes, he defeated Flavio Abbate and Giulio Graziotti at the BNL Italy Major.

His competitive journey has continued internationally. In 2026, Carlos is ranked seventh in the Asia Pacific Padel Tour men's ranking, with victories including the APPT Love All Grand Slam Platinum in Singapore. For Neuron, a player with that level of experience provides something invaluable: constant feedback from competition, where control, response, confidence and consistency matter on every point.

Mattia Settimi: building the next step

The other side of the project is the future.

Italian player Mattia Settimi is competing internationally on the FIP Promises Tour. As of October 12, 2026, he sits at No. 47 in the Boys Under 14 ranking with 923 points.

His 2026 season already includes a final in Bali, semi-finals in Marino and Frascati, and quarter-final appearances in Elda, Paredes and Hamburg.

For a developing player, every tournament adds another piece: new opponents, different conditions and more experience under pressure.

Zara Settimi: competing beyond her category

Zara Settimi is another name already making an impact on the international junior circuit.

She currently sits at No. 11 in the FIP Promises Girls Under 12 ranking and is also competing in the Under 14 category.

One of the standout moments of her season came in Jakarta, where Zara and Chikage Yasuda won both the Under 12 and Under 14 titles without dropping a set. She has also collected an Under 12 title in Paredes and another victory in Bali.

Moving between categories and competing internationally at such an early stage makes every tournament part of a much longer story.

Carlos, Mattia and Zara represent three very different moments in the life of a competitive player.

One brings professional experience. Two are building their path through the international junior circuit.

For Neuron Padel, that creates an opportunity to develop alongside players rather than simply appear beside them.

Because equipment is not only designed in a workshop.

It evolves every time it enters the court.
