Cookies & browser storage

Updated 27 October 2026

Provider: Alessandro Settimi, sole trader trading as Euro Padel. For cookie and privacy questions: europadel@yahoo.com. See the privacy notice for full business details.

We use browser storage to keep your basket and make checkout work reliably. Optional external videos are disabled until you allow them. You can shop and pay without accepting optional content.

· Privacy notice

Your choices

Choose “Reject optional”, “Accept optional” or “Configure” in the banner. Optional external media is off by default. Browsing, scrolling or closing preferences does not give consent. The Privacy settings link at the bottom of every page lets you change or withdraw your choice at any time. Withdrawal stops embedded videos from loading and unloads videos already open on this page.

Choices are stored on this browser for up to 180 days. You may be asked again when this period ends, you clear site data or we materially change the optional services. Other browsers and devices have their own choices. We cannot remove cookies previously set on a third party’s domain; you can clear those in your browser settings.

Storage used by the shop

Local storage and session storage are not cookies, but both store information in your browser. No advertising or analytics trackers are currently installed in the shop.

Email confirmation codes and their short-lived checkout authorisation are held in page memory, not saved to local storage or session storage. Reloading the page requires email verification again; an existing checkout request reference is preserved for safe retries.

Payments and delivery of the site

Stripe’s hosted payment page is opened only when you proceed to payment. Stripe manages its own storage, security and fraud prevention on that page. Our optional media choice does not control Stripe’s payment page. See Stripe’s privacy policy and cookie policy.

The shop and API use Vercel and Render hosting, and product images may be served by Cloudinary. These services receive network information needed to serve your request. Fonts and the React/Babel libraries are served from our own website. This banner controls optional media, not essential hosting or purchase processing.

Browser controls

You can remove stored site data in your browser. Removing essential storage can empty your basket or interrupt checkout recovery. Rejecting optional media here does not delete your basket. This notice covers the public shop; the protected administration area has separate authentication and security storage.
