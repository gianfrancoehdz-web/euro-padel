Returns & product issues

Updated 27 October 2026

A return because you changed your mind and a claim about faulty goods follow different rules. Contact europadel@yahoo.com with your order reference so we can help.

Changed your mind?

For consumer online purchases, notify us within 14 calendar days after receiving your goods. You do not need to give a reason. Send the goods back within 14 days of notification. Direct return costs are yours for this type of return. This does not apply to the necessary costs of a valid conformity claim.

Use the withdrawal instructions and optional form in our terms. Pack the goods safely with accessories and any available original packaging. Reasonable inspection does not itself remove your right to withdraw. See the terms for reimbursement timing and any deduction for handling beyond what is needed to inspect the goods.

Faulty or incorrectly described goods?

Contact the seller, Euro Padel, with a description of the issue and your order reference or other proof of purchase. If useful, you may include product photos. Do not send identity-document copies or card details.

  1. Tell us what is wrong and when you noticed it.
  2. We assess the issue and arrange the appropriate remedy under the applicable consumer rules.
  3. Follow the return or inspection arrangements provided by customer service.

Applicable consumer remedies and necessary costs are explained in the statutory rights section of our terms. This is separate from a return because you changed your mind.
