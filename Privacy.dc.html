Privacy notice

Updated 29 October 2026

Optional address suggestions

When you type at least three characters in the checkout address field, the entered address text and selected country are sent through our server to Geoapify to suggest matching addresses. We do not add your contact name, email, telephone or apartment details to these requests. You can always enter your address manually. Suggestions do not guarantee delivery or replace checking your house number and postal code. See Geoapify’s privacy information.

Information used for your order

When you order, we process your name, email address, telephone number, delivery address, country, basket contents, discount code where provided, order references and payment status. These details are needed to prepare and perform your purchase, arrange delivery and provide order support. Accounting and transaction records may also be processed to meet applicable legal obligations.

Where email verification is requested at checkout, we send a short-lived confirmation code to the email address you provide, to check that you can receive order messages and limit misuse. Verification records use protected hashes rather than plaintext codes or email addresses. Codes expire after 10 minutes and a successful verification authorises one checkout attempt for up to 20 minutes. These are transactional messages, not marketing or account registration.

Technical access information, including IP address, request times and security events, is processed to run the website and prevent abuse, based on our legitimate interest in protecting the shop and customers. We do not use this information for advertising profiling.

Payments

Payment details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout. The shop receives transaction references, amounts and payment outcomes, not your full card number or security code. Stripe processes payment and fraud-prevention information under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Service providers

We use Vercel for the storefront, Render for the backend and database, Cloudinary for product images, Resend for transactional email and Stripe for payments. Delivery details are shared with the carrier handling your order when required for delivery. Order information may also be disclosed to professional advisers or public authorities when legally required.

These providers operate internationally and may process information outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy and data-processing terms describe processing locations and applicable transfer arrangements: Vercel, Render, Cloudinary, Resend and Stripe. Contact the controller for information about the safeguards applicable to your data, including a copy where available. External videos use YouTube only with your permission, as explained in our storage notice.

Storage and optional content

Essential browser storage supports your basket, checkout recovery and privacy preferences. External videos require your consent. We do not currently operate analytics or advertising trackers. Read the cookies and storage notice or . Refusing optional media does not prevent a purchase.

How long information is kept

Business correspondence, accounting records and supporting commercial documents are retained for six years from the last relevant accounting entry, subject to any different legal period or a need to preserve records for outstanding claims. This period does not apply indiscriminately to all browsing information.

Order and support information is kept while needed to fulfil the purchase, resolve enquiries, manage returns and deal with applicable claims or legal obligations. Technical logs are kept for the period needed to operate the service, investigate security incidents and meet applicable obligations. Retention is determined by those purposes and any relevant limitation periods, rather than permanent use for marketing. Browser storage lifetimes are listed in the storage notice. Withdrawing optional media consent does not require deletion of records that must still be retained by law.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing by writing to europadel@yahoo.com. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. A copy of your identity document is not routinely required; proportionate additional information may be requested if there are reasonable doubts about your identity.

You can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your competent supervisory authority.

Order and dispatch messages are transactional. Buying a product or accepting optional media does not subscribe you to marketing. We do not operate automated decisions about customer eligibility with legal or similarly significant effects.
