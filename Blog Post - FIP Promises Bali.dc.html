FIP Promises Bali 2026 Comes to Indonesia

The FIP Promises Bali 2026 is set to bring international junior padel to Indonesia, part of the FIP Tour organized under the International Padel Federation. The tournament runs from August 10 to 16 at Island Sports — Holywings Padel Club, in Bali.

The event is backed by PB YPI Bali and sponsors including Bullpadel, among others, bringing a high-level competitive field to the island for a full week of matches.

The standout storyline for the BLUWX community: Zara Settimi is heading to Indonesia to compete in the tournament, going head to head with Bellaluna on one of this edition's most anticipated cards.

Zara has been building her name on the international junior circuit, and Bali marks another step in that journey, competing against strong regional talent on foreign courts.

Follow along and cheer Zara on as she takes on the FIP Promises Bali 2026 field.
