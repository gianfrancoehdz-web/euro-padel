Comunidad de Madrid Premier Padel P1 by Oysho — Movistar Arena, September 2026

The Comunidad de Madrid Premier Padel P1 by Oysho returns for its 5th consecutive edition, backed by the International Padel Federation, as Madrid once again becomes the epicenter of world padel.

The event runs across two stages: qualifying takes place August 29-31 at Club Pro Padel Coslada (Av. de la Industria 3, Coslada, Madrid), before the main draw moves to the Movistar Arena (Av. Felipe II, 28009 Madrid) from September 1 to 6.

Total prize money for this edition reaches € 479,068.00, played on indoor courts with Mondo surface, built by MejorSet. Matches are played with official Wilson Premier Padel balls (men) and Wilson Premier Padel S (women).

The main draw features 48 men (37 direct entries, 8 qualifiers, 3 wildcards) and 28 women (23 direct entries, 4 qualifiers, 1 wildcard).
